Post a reading

Two steps, both work from your phone. You need to be signed in to GitHub.

  1. Upload your photo

    Pick the photo of your spread. Big phone photos are fine; the site shrinks them and strips location data automatically.

    Upload photo

    Note the file name it shows, for example IMG_4021.jpg.

  2. Write the reading

    This opens a new entry already filled in with today's date. Change the title, the cards and the photo name, write your notes, then press Commit changes.

    New daily reading New challenge entry

    Your site updates about a minute after you commit.
